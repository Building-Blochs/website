02 / Teach with it

One ball, a whole
lesson on qubits.

The Orb is a vector picture you can hold. Anchor a class on "the arrow is the state, every gate is a rotation" and you can teach superposition, phase and non-commutativity without a single complex number.

Sample lesson · 45 minutes

A class, gate by gate

adapt freely →
1

What's a qubit? · 0–5 min

Arrow up is |0⟩, arrow down is |1⟩ — just like a classical bit. Hand the ball around. Make it concrete before any maths.

2

Superposition & phase — Level 1 · 5–15 min

Press H: the arrow jumps to the equator — equal parts 0 and 1. Press it again and it returns. Introduce Z as a flip of phase.

3

Smaller rotations — Level 2 · 15–25 min

Add S and T. Students see that gates come in different "amounts" of rotation, not just half-turns.

4

Order matters — Level 3 · 25–35 min

Use the 90° rotations X₉₀, Y₉₀, Z₉₀. Have a volunteer try H then Z and then Z then H — the arrow lands in different places. That's non-commutativity.

5

Free play & recap — Level 4 · 35–45 min

Level 4 revisits H and S as a longer puzzle. Let students race to flip the arrow, then recap: state = arrow, gate = rotation, measurement = the part the Orb can't show.

Know your room

Framing it for any age

KIDS · 8–12

Skip the names

Talk about "the arrow". Let them solve Level 1 by trial and error, and celebrate the confetti screen loudly.

HIGH SCHOOL

Anchor on the vector

The Bloch sphere is just a 3D arrow; gates are rotations. No complex numbers needed for the picture.

ADULTS

Lead with "what's different"

Start from "what makes a qubit different from a bit" rather than the maths. The Orb is your answer.

PHYSICS / CS

Go straight to unitaries

Gates as unitary rotations; point at the axes on the buttons and discuss why H is a 180° turn about the (x+z) axis.

Before the bell rings

Classroom troubleshooting

⟳

90° rotations feel wrong

The Orb may be wired left-handed (chirality). Start the software with --invert-y-axis and test before class.

⏻

Phantom gate presses

On Xbox-style controllers, press the middle metal button until the red ring is on — otherwise the analog sticks mimic the buttons.

🌐

Texts are in Dutch

The built-in level texts default to Dutch. Switch to the templates/en/ folder, or translate on the fly.

↻

A level keeps reloading

Known bug. Open a second browser tab on the controller test page — it tends to settle things down.
