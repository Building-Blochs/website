Why teach with the sphere

← the short version

  • Abstract maths, made physical

    A single arrow anyone can read — you can start teaching superposition and gates with nothing on the board.

  • Hands-on, not hands-off

    Students hold the ball and turn the arrow themselves. People remember what they do far better than what they're told.

  • Gates you can see

    Every quantum gate is just a rotation of the arrow, so superposition and phase finally click as a visible movement instead of rows of symbols.

  • Scientifically honest

    It's the real Bloch-sphere representation researchers use every day — a faithful picture, not a watered-down metaphor.

  • One model, every audience

    The very same ball explains superposition to a curious eight-year-old and unitary rotations to physics undergrads.

  • Ready to teach, today

    We give you a ready-made lesson plan, so there's little to prepare — walk in and teach.
