Open-source quantum demonstrator

A qubit you can actually touch.

The Building Blochs project allows anyone to build their own life-sized qubit. This motorised ball is a great tool to explain quantum computing and to illustrate what happens during a quantum gate. Build one, teach with it, or take it to a science festival.

How the Orb works

no maths required — just watch the arrow
|0⟩

Start at the pole

The arrow points straight up. That's the qubit in state zero — a plain classical bit.

H

Apply a gate

Press H and the arrow swings to the equator: an equal superposition of 0 and 1.

|1⟩

Read the rotation

Every gate is just a rotation on the sphere. Nothing collapses — you simply watch it move.

What it is

The simplest quantum computer…
that isn't a quantum computer.

One qubit, drawn as a 3D arrow on a sphere. You can explain superposition to an eight-year-old in thirty seconds, then go deep on unitary rotations with physics students — using the very same ball.
